Flagship · AI Engineering

Compliance RAG Assistant

A production-grade agentic retrieval system that answers questions on GDPR, the EU AI Act, and UK data protection law, with every answer grounded in source documents and cited. Combines hybrid search (BM25 + vector with reciprocal rank fusion), cross-encoder reranking, and an LLM agent that decides when to retrieve, compare regulations, or flag low-confidence answers.
